The marketing funnel describes how audiences move from awareness to consideration, conversion, retention, and advocacy. Awareness tactics include social media, SEO content, influencer collaborations, PR, and educational content. Consideration tactics include comparison pages, webinars, guides, testimonials, case studies when verified, and email nurturing. Conversion tactics include landing page optimization, clear offers, risk reducers, retargeting, and sales enablement.

Retention and advocacy depend on onboarding, lifecycle email, loyalty programs, customer education, community, referral programs, and feedback loops. Each funnel stage needs different KPIs: awareness may track reach and engagement; consideration may track CTR, content engagement, and qualified leads; conversion may track conversion rate, CAC, CPA, and ROAS; retention may track repeat purchase and churn.
