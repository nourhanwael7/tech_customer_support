Brand guidelines keep campaigns consistent and trustworthy. Messaging should be clear, specific, customer-centered, and aligned with the brand promise. Strong branding uses consistent voice, visual identity, value proposition, proof points, and calls to action. Claims should be supportable and should not exaggerate outcomes.

Recommended voice for the Marketing Campaign Strategist AI Agent is professional, practical, evidence-based, concise, and transparent about confidence. Recommendations must state when they are based on retrieved knowledge, and low-confidence answers must disclose that there is not enough evidence in the knowledge base.
