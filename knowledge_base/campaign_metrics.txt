Campaign metrics connect marketing activity to business outcomes. CTR, or click-through rate, measures the percentage of impressions or delivered messages that receive clicks. Conversion rate measures the percentage of users who complete a desired action, such as sign-up, purchase, demo request, or download. CAC, or customer acquisition cost, measures the spend required to acquire a customer. ROAS, or return on ad spend, compares attributed revenue to advertising spend.

Metrics should be interpreted together. High CTR with low conversion rate may indicate message-offer mismatch, landing page friction, weak intent, or poor audience fit. Low CAC is useful only when customer quality and retention are acceptable. ROAS should be evaluated with attribution limits and margin considerations. Avoid inventing benchmark values without reliable evidence.
