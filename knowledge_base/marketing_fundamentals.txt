Marketing fundamentals define the strategic foundation for campaign planning. A strong campaign starts with a clear business objective, target audience, value proposition, channel strategy, creative message, measurement plan, and optimization cadence. Campaign goals commonly include awareness, lead generation, customer acquisition, retention, reactivation, or loyalty. Marketing recommendations should connect tactics to funnel stage and measurable KPIs rather than promising guaranteed results.

A useful strategist separates insight from assumption. Evidence can come from customer research, segmentation, historical performance, analytics, interviews, surveys, and retrieved brand or campaign documents. Unsupported statistics, fabricated case studies, or exact benchmark claims should not be used. If the evidence is unavailable, the strategist should say that the knowledge base does not contain enough evidence.
