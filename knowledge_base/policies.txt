TECHSTORE COMPANY POLICIES

1. REFUND AND RETURN POLICY
1.1 Standard Returns: Customers may return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be in original, undamaged condition with all original packaging and accessories.
1.2 Electronics: Opened electronics can only be returned if defective. A 15% restocking fee applies to non-defective opened electronics.
1.3 Refund Timeline: Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days of receiving the return. Credit card refunds may take an additional 3-5 days to appear on statements.
1.4 Non-Returnable Items: Software products, digital downloads, consumables (batteries, ink cartridges), and personalized items are non-returnable.
1.5 Damaged Items: Items damaged during shipping must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with photographic evidence.

2. SHIPPING POLICY
2.1 Processing Time: Orders are processed within 1-2 business days. Orders placed on weekends or holidays are processed the next business day.
2.2 Shipping Methods: Standard (5-7 days), Express (2-3 days), Overnight (next business day for orders before 2PM EST).
2.3 Free Shipping: Available on all orders over $50 for standard shipping within the contiguous US.
2.4 Shipping Confirmation: Customers receive email confirmation with tracking information when orders ship.
2.5 Lost Packages: Claims for lost packages must be filed within 14 days of the expected delivery date. TechStore will work with carriers to resolve claims within 7-10 business days.

3. CUSTOMER SERVICE POLICY
3.1 Response Times: Live chat responses within 2 minutes, email responses within 24 hours, phone hold times under 5 minutes during business hours.
3.2 Escalation: Unresolved issues after initial contact are escalated to senior support within 2 business days.
3.3 Compensation Policy: For significant service failures, TechStore may offer store credit, expedited shipping on next order, or partial refunds as goodwill gestures.
3.4 Harassment Policy: TechStore maintains a zero-tolerance policy for abusive or threatening behavior toward staff. Service may be terminated for violations.

4. PRIVACY POLICY
4.1 Data Collection: TechStore collects name, email, phone, address, payment info, and browsing/purchase history.
4.2 Data Use: Data is used to process orders, personalize shopping, send communications, and improve services.
4.3 Data Sharing: Customer data is never sold. It may be shared with payment processors, shipping carriers, and analytics providers under strict agreements.
4.4 Data Retention: Account data is retained for 3 years after last activity. Transaction records are kept for 7 years for tax purposes.
4.5 Opt-Out: Customers may opt out of marketing communications at any time. Core transactional emails cannot be disabled.

5. FRAUD PREVENTION POLICY
5.1 Order Verification: High-value orders or orders with mismatched billing/shipping addresses may require additional verification.
5.2 Account Suspension: Accounts with suspected fraudulent activity will be suspended pending investigation.
5.3 Chargeback Policy: Customers should contact TechStore directly before initiating chargebacks. Abuse of chargeback processes may result in account suspension.

6. WARRANTY POLICY
6.1 Standard Warranty: All products include at minimum a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering manufacturing defects.
6.2 Extended Warranty: TechStore Protection Plans available for 2 or 3 years, covering accidental damage and technical failures.
6.3 Warranty Claims: Contact manufacturer directly for warranty issues or contact TechStore support for assistance with the process.
6.4 Exclusions: Warranties do not cover physical damage, water damage, unauthorized modifications, or normal wear and tear.
