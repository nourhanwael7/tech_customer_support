Digital marketing uses channels such as SEO, paid search, email marketing, social media, content marketing, landing pages, and retargeting. SEO supports durable discovery by matching content to search intent, improving technical health, strengthening metadata, and publishing useful content for each funnel stage. Paid search captures high-intent demand but requires close monitoring of spend, conversion rate, cost per acquisition, and return on ad spend.

Email marketing is effective for lifecycle nurturing, onboarding, promotions, reactivation, and retention when messages are segmented and relevant. Social media is useful for awareness, community engagement, creator content, social proof, and user-generated content. Digital campaigns should align creative message, offer, landing page, and audience targeting to reduce drop-off between click and conversion.
