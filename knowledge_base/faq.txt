FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS - TechStore Customer Support

Q: What is your return policy?
A: We offer a 30-day return policy on all items in original condition with receipt. Electronics must be unopened or defective to qualify. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item.

Q: How long does shipping take?
A: Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping takes 2-3 business days. Overnight shipping is available for orders placed before 2 PM EST. Free standard shipping is available on orders over $50.

Q: How do I track my order?
A: You can track your order using the tracking number provided in your shipping confirmation email. Visit our website and enter your tracking number in the "Track Order" section, or use the carrier's website directly.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and TechStore Gift Cards. All transactions are secured with 256-bit SSL encryption.

Q: Do you offer price matching?
A: Yes, we offer price matching within 14 days of purchase if you find the same item at a lower price from an authorized retailer. Contact customer support with the competitor's price and we'll match it.

Q: How do I cancel an order?
A: Orders can be cancelled within 2 hours of placement by contacting customer support. Once an order has shipped, it cannot be cancelled — you would need to initiate a return after receiving it.

Q: What is your warranty policy?
A: All products come with a minimum 1-year manufacturer warranty. TechStore also offers extended warranty plans (2-year and 3-year) for additional protection against defects.

Q: How do I contact customer support?
A: You can reach us via: Live Chat (Mon-Fri 8AM-10PM EST), Email (support@techstore.com), Phone 1-800-TECH-HELP (Mon-Sun 9AM-6PM EST), or this AI support system.

Q: Can I change my shipping address after placing an order?
A: Address changes can be made within 1 hour of order placement by contacting customer support. After that, we cannot guarantee the address can be updated as orders enter the fulfillment process quickly.

Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: Currently, we ship to the US, Canada, UK, Australia, and select EU countries. International orders may be subject to customs duties and taxes which are the buyer's responsibility.

Q: How do I reset my account password?
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your email address, and follow the instructions sent to your inbox. If you don't receive an email within 5 minutes, check your spam folder.

Q: Are gift cards available?
A: Yes, digital and physical gift cards are available in denominations of $25, $50, $100, and $200. Digital gift cards are delivered via email within minutes. Physical cards ship within 3-5 days.
